## Chosen approach: Workshop Ledger

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial industrial design, inspired by architectural journals, Indian modernism, and the visual language of workshop ledgers.

### Core Principles
1. **Craft before decoration:** details of joinery, material, and finish carry the visual story.
2. **Warm industrial contrast:** charcoal steel is softened by ivory paper tones and a restrained brass signature.
3. **Editorial asymmetry:** layouts use offset columns, ruled lines, and deliberate whitespace instead of uniform centered cards.
4. **Local, direct, useful:** actions and contact information stay visible and immediate on every device.

### Color Philosophy
Charcoal anchors trust and structural strength. Warm ivory creates the feel of a well-kept project ledger rather than a sterile catalog. Brass is used sparingly as an ownable signal of care, finish, and premium detailing. A muted clay accent adds a human, workshop-made warmth without becoming decorative noise.

### Layout Paradigm
A left-anchored editorial composition with an offset hero copy block, narrow section labels, and a gallery that alternates between large feature images and compact supporting tiles. Mobile collapses into a clean reading rail with persistent contact actions.

### Signature Elements
- Thin brass rules and numbered section labels inspired by architectural drawings.
- Framed image windows with small caption strips, like project sheets.
- A circular NLS mark used as a visual stamp in navigation and contact areas.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel tactile and decisive: buttons compress slightly, links reveal a brass underline, gallery tiles lift by a few pixels, and the header keeps the call action within thumb reach.

### Animation
Use 180–260ms ease-out transitions for buttons, image scale, and nav underlines. On first load, reveal the hero copy and project stamp with a short upward motion; stagger service rows by 50ms. Respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid looping decorative animations.

### Typography System
Display: **DM Serif Display** for expressive, architectural headlines. Body and metadata: **Manrope** for clean legibility and a contemporary workshop feel. Use uppercase Manrope with generous tracking for labels, and DM Serif Display with tight leading for major statements.

### Brand Essence
New Laxmi Steel is a dependable fabrication studio for homeowners, builders, and businesses who want structural work with considered finish — not off-the-shelf metalwork. Personality: **precise, grounded, quietly ambitious**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines sound confident and material-led. CTAs are direct, local, and low-friction. Microcopy explains what happens next rather than overselling.

Example lines:
- “Made to hold the view.”
- “Share your measurements. We’ll shape the right finish.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use the generated NLS geometric beam-and-glass mark beside a custom-styled stacked wordmark: NEW LAXMI on the first line, STEEL on the second, with compact tracking and a small brass rule.

### Signature Brand Color
**Ledger Brass — #B58A55**, used for rules, active states, and the NLS stamp.
